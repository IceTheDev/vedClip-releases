Instant replay clipping for Windows. Press a hotkey, keep the moment.

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/IceTheDev/vedClip-releases/releases/latest)**: run `vedClip-win-Setup.exe`. After that, vedClip updates itself.

> vedClip is in beta. Thank you for helping test it.

vedClip is an instant-replay clipper for Windows. It keeps the last minute or two of your game or screen in memory, and when you press a key it saves that moment as a clip. Then you can trim it, mix the audio tracks, add captions, make a vertical version for TikTok, Shorts or Reels, and drag it into Discord.

## Install

1. Download `vedClip-win-Setup.exe` from the latest release on this page.
2. Run it. Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box because the beta is not signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. vedClip opens and starts recording. Closing the window keeps it recording in the tray (the small icons by the clock). Use **Exit** in the tray menu to quit.

It updates itself. When a new version is ready you will see a banner, and you can restart to update.

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- A graphics card that can encode H.264 video (almost every NVIDIA, AMD and Intel GPU from the last several years)
- A few GB of free disk space for clips

## First steps

- The default clip key is shown in Settings > Hotkey. Press it to save the last stretch. Press it again quickly to extend the same clip.
- In Settings > Recording, add your games to **Games and apps to capture**. vedClip records the one that is open and goes back to your desktop when none are.
- In Settings > Clips & Storage you can pick the clip length, quality and where clips are saved.
- Click a clip in the Library to open the editor. Captions, the vertical preview and Share are in there.

## Good to know

- Some games or anti-cheat programs do not allow capture tools. Check your game's rules before using vedClip in competitive or protected games.
- vedClip is built to stay light, but it is a beta. If a game stutters while it runs, please tell us.
- Everything stays on your PC. Read Settings > About > Privacy for the details.

## Found a problem?

1. Open Settings > Diagnostics > **Export diagnostics** and save the zip. It contains logs and hardware info, never video or audio.
2. Tell us what you did, what you expected, and what happened, and send the zip along. A screenshot helps.

If the app shows an error with an ID that looks like `VDC-1234ABCD`, include that ID.
